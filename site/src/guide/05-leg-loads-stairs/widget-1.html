Pose

Joint angles: you set the hip and knee. Auto: you set the stair and where the body is, and inverse kinematics (IK, working back from where the feet must be to the joint angles; page 01) finds the angles.
+ = thigh swung forward.
Ankle pitch follows (= knee − hip) to keep the foot flat under an upright pelvis.
The height of one stair step. Requirement R12 in the repo: 17–18 cm risers, 25 cm treads (the part you stand on).
0 = pelvis still at floor height, front foot just placed; 1 = standing on the stair.
Hip axis above the sole in the walking crouch; 0.694 m in the straight stance.
Sole runs from −0.09 m (heel) to +0.15 m (toe). The repo's worst pose has −0.062 m.
