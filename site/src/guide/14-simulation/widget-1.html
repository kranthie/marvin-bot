Contact model

How hard the floor's spring pushes back per metre of sinking (log scale). Stiffer = less sinking, but needs smaller timesteps.
How much the floor soaks up the impact, like a shock absorber. 0 = perfectly bouncy.
Roughly how long the floor takes to push the body back out. Shorter = harder floor.
1 = settles without bouncing; below 1 bounces, above 1 is sluggish.
Defaults 0.02 s and 1; impedance from solimp 0.9 → 0.95 over 1 mm (linear here).
Fraction of the impact speed kept in the bounce: 0 = lands dead, 1 = bounces back to full height.

Time stepping

How far time jumps each step (log scale). Smaller = more accurate but more steps to compute.
The hard constraint always uses semi-implicit Euler with an impulse.
