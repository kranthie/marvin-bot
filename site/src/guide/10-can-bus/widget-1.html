Physical layer

RobStride's motors use classic CAN; FD is shown as an alternative for other motors.
Classic CAN tops out at 1 Mbit/s; FD uses this rate for arbitration.
Only the data phase speeds up. The Orin's controllers support up to 15 Mbit/s with a suitable transceiver[8].

Frame

RobStride commands and replies carry 8 bytes. FD allows 12-64 in steps.
100% = the exact worst case a scheduler must plan for; real traffic is usually lower but data-dependent.
