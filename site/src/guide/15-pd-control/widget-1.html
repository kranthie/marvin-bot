Link and gravity

Knee: shin, ankle and foot, 3.42 kg, 0.184 kg·m² (from the URDF).
Torque gravity puts on the joint with the link horizontal. Knee with the foot off the ground: 3.42 × 9.81 × 0.205 m = 6.9 N·m.

Motor

How hard the motor's own spinning rotor is to speed up, measured at the motor shaft, before the gearbox.
Motor turns per joint turn. RobStride RS04: 9. The armature grows with its square.
Most torque the motor can give, briefly (RS04: 120 N·m).
Top joint speed; the faster the joint turns, the less torque is left (RS04: 20.9 rad/s = 200 rpm).
Dry friction torque in the gearbox that always resists motion.

Gains

The spring: torque per radian of error. Higher = snappier, less sag (log scale).
The shock absorber: torque per rad/s of speed. Higher = less swinging past the target (log scale).
