Nothing on this page is built, tested or verified. Marvin Bot is in its simulation phase. The e-stop chain, watchdog, fuses, battery protection and software layers described here are design requirements from docs/safety.md; each must be built and verified on the bench before anyone relies on it. The playgrounds are teaching models with simplified physics, not a risk assessment. Marvin Bot does not claim compliance with any standard named here. If you build or run a robot like this, you need your own risk assessment, the component makers' instructions and the rules that apply where you are, and you do it at your own risk.
