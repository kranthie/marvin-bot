How to read this picture: three bars on a logarithmic scale (each grid step is about three times the last): the estimated force on top, then the body region's two limits. Red top bar = over the limit, green = under. "Quasi-static" means slow pressing or clamping (the body cannot move away); "transient" means a brief knock where the body can move. "Robot effective mass" is how heavy the limb feels at the contact point. "Speed for the threshold" is the highest speed that would stay under the limit.
