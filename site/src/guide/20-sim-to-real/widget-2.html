Reading the numbers: a good model has a speed residual close to the noise floor (you cannot do better than the sensor's own noise), a small worst parameter error, and a small sim-to-real gap, meaning the simulator's prediction and the real joint agree. The straight-line chart under the speed chart is a separate test with the output clamped, which measures Kt (torque per amp) from the slope.
