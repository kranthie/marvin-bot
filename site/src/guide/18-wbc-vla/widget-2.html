How to read this picture: time runs left to right over three seconds. Inference: one bar per call, on two alternating rows so overlapping calls stay visible (blue = thinking, violet = network, amber = random extra delay; the white tick is when the image was taken). Executing: what the arms do, green while a chunk plays (two shades tell successive chunks apart), red while they hold still waiting. Action age: one dot per action, showing how old the image behind it was. 50 Hz control: the walking controller's ticks, which never stop. Less red and lower dots are better.
