    Skills (scripted, not the trained policy)

    Load: green below 70% of the motor's continuous (rated) torque, amber at 70–100%, red above it: more than it can hold for long without overheating, fine for a few seconds (the peak rating is about 3× higher). Movement: a motor glows brighter the faster it turns (fully bright from about 90° per second); a motor that is holding still is drawn dark. The table below gives each joint's speed.
