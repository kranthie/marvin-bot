238 terms used across the playgrounds, in plain language. Each entry links to the pages that use it.

## A

- <span id="abduction"></span>**Abduction**: Moving a limb away from the body's midline (positive roll on Marvin Bot). <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/)</span>
- <span id="accelerometer"></span>**Accelerometer**: A sensor that measures gravity's pull plus the body's own acceleration, so at rest it tells which way is down. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="action"></span>**Action**: What the policy outputs at each step. For Marvin Bot, one number per joint: a target angle offset from the standing pose, which a fast PD loop in each motor then tries to reach. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/), [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="action-chunk"></span>**Action chunk**: A short sequence of future actions output at once by a policy, executed while the next chunk is computed. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="action-scale"></span>**Action scale**: The factor converting the policy's output into a joint-angle offset from the stance; 0.5 rad per unit for Marvin Bot. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="active-stereo"></span>**Active stereo**: Stereo depth with an infrared projector adding a dot pattern, so that textureless surfaces such as plain floors can still be matched. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="actuator"></span>**Actuator**: A motor with its gearbox, encoder and driver electronics in one unit. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/)</span>
- <span id="actuator-bench"></span>**Actuator bench**: A test rig for single actuators. It measures torque, speed, heating and communication timing before many units are bought. <span class="on">On: [22 Build phases and roadmap](../22-roadmap/)</span>
- <span id="actuator-delay"></span>**Actuator delay**: Time between a command being computed and the motor acting on it; Marvin Bot's training randomises 0-10 ms. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="actuator-network"></span>**Actuator network**: A small neural network trained on bench data to predict an actuator's torque from recent commands and motion, used in simulation instead of an ideal motor model. <span class="on">On: [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="adaptive-sampling"></span>**Adaptive sampling**: Starting training episodes more often at the parts of the clips where the policy has been failing. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="advantage"></span>**Advantage**: During training: how much better an action turned out than expected. A second network (the critic) predicts how well things should go from a situation; if the action did better than that, it gets a positive advantage and becomes more likely next time. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="ahrs"></span>**AHRS**: Attitude and heading reference system: an IMU (motion sensor) with its own built-in processing, so it reports the body's tilt and heading directly instead of raw rotation rates and accelerations. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="aliasing"></span>**Aliasing**: When a signal is sampled too slowly, fast vibrations masquerade as slow ones; sampling fast and averaging prevents it. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="anchor-body"></span>**Anchor body**: The body (usually the torso) whose pose is tracked globally; the other bodies are compared relative to it, so drift in position is not counted as pose error. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="ankle-strategy"></span>**Ankle strategy**: Balancing by shifting pressure between heel and toes with ankle torque, without stepping. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/)</span>
- <span id="anthropometry"></span>**Anthropometry**: The measurement of human body dimensions. Tables give segment lengths as fractions of standing height. <span class="on">On: [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/)</span>
- <span id="arbitration"></span>**Arbitration**: How CAN decides who talks when two devices start at once: each sends its identifier number bit by bit, and the device with the lower number wins and carries on, while the other waits. Nothing is garbled. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="armature"></span>**Armature**: The motor rotor's inertia as felt at the joint (the reflected inertia: rotor inertia times the gear ratio squared). Simulators add it to the joint's own inertia; Marvin Bot's values are placeholders until the phase 2 bench measures them. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/), [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="asymmetric-actor-critic"></span>**Asymmetric actor-critic**: A training trick: the policy that will run on the robot (the actor) sees only what real sensors can measure, while the helper network that grades it during training (the critic) is allowed to see simulator-only facts such as the true speed or the exact terrain. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>

## B

- <span id="back-emf"></span>**Back-EMF**: The voltage a spinning motor generates against its supply, proportional to speed. It sets the top speed for a given battery voltage. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>
- <span id="backdrivability"></span>**Backdrivability**: How easily the output can be turned by an external force. Backdrivable actuators absorb impacts and can sense torque from motor current. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>
- <span id="baseline"></span>**Baseline**: The distance between a stereo camera's two cameras; a longer baseline gives smaller depth error at range. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="bending-moment"></span>**Bending moment**: The turning effect of a load about a section of a beam: force times distance, in N·m. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="bias"></span>**Bias**: A slowly drifting offset in a sensor's reading; integrated gyro bias makes an angle estimate drift without limit. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="bill-of-materials"></span><b>Bill of materials (BOM)</b>: The complete list of parts, quantities and prices needed to build a product. Marvin Bot's is the spreadsheet bom/bom.xlsx. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="bit-stuffing"></span>**Bit stuffing**: A CAN rule: after five identical bits in a row, the sender inserts one opposite bit so receivers can keep their clocks in step. It means a frame's length depends slightly on the data it carries. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="bms"></span>**BMS**: Battery management system: electronics that watch every cell's voltage and temperature, balance them and disconnect the pack on a fault. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="bought-part"></span>**Bought part**: A part bought finished, such as an actuator, camera or bearing. Its cost is the price on a listing. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="bus-load"></span>**Bus load**: The fraction of time a bus is busy transmitting frames. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>

## C

- <span id="c-rate"></span>**C-rate**: Current divided by a cell's capacity: 1C empties it in an hour, 10C in six minutes. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="can-bus"></span>**CAN bus**: Controller Area Network: a two-wire differential bus on which every node hears every frame and identifiers set priority; RobStride actuators use classic CAN at 1 Mbit/s. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/), [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="can-fd"></span>**CAN FD**: CAN with flexible data rate: the data phase runs faster than arbitration and frames carry up to 64 bytes. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="capture-point"></span>**Capture point**: The spot on the ground where the robot would have to step (or put its centre of pressure) to come to a complete stop. The faster the body is moving, the further ahead that spot is. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/)</span>
- <span id="carrier-board"></span>**Carrier board**: The board a compute module such as a Jetson plugs into. It provides the connectors and power. A developer kit is a module already on a carrier board. <span class="on">On: [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/), [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="centre-of-mass"></span>**Centre of mass**: The mass-weighted average position of all a robot's parts; gravity acts as if all the weight were there. Balance depends on keeping it over the feet. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/), [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/)</span>
- <span id="centre-of-pressure"></span>**Centre of pressure**: The single point where the ground's distributed push on the soles can be treated as acting. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/)</span>
- <span id="chirp-signal"></span>**Chirp signal**: A test input whose frequency sweeps from low to high, used to excite a system across its frequency range. <span class="on">On: [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="class-t-fuse"></span>**Class-T fuse**: A fast, current-limiting fuse with a high DC interrupting rating, used to break a hard short of a lithium pack safely. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="closed-kinematic-chain"></span>**Closed kinematic chain**: A set of links and joints that forms a loop, so the joint angles are not independent; most simulators need an extra constraint to handle it. <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/)</span>
- <span id="code-cad"></span>**Code-CAD**: Writing mechanical parts as programs that build solids from primitives and boolean operations, so they are parametric, diffable and can feed other tools directly. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="coefficient-of-friction"></span>**Coefficient of friction**: μ, the ratio of the largest sideways friction force to the pressing force at a contact; soft rubber has a higher μ than hard plastic. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="coefficient-of-restitution"></span>**Coefficient of restitution**: Ratio of rebound speed to impact speed; 0 means no bounce, 1 a perfectly elastic bounce. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="collision-geometry"></span>**Collision geometry**: Simple shapes the physics engine uses to detect contact; kept simple because contact checks run every step. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="collision-sweep"></span>**Collision sweep**: Moving each joint through its range in software and checking for overlaps between parts, to set joint limits and find packaging errors. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="complementary-filter"></span>**Complementary filter**: A simple way to estimate tilt from an IMU: trust the gyroscope for quick changes (it is smooth but slowly drifts) and the accelerometer's sense of "down" for the long-term average (it is noisy but does not drift). <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="constraint-solver"></span>**Constraint solver**: The part of a physics engine that works out all the contact and joint forces at once each step, so that feet do not sink into the floor and joints stay connected. PGS, TGS and MuJoCo's Newton solver are different methods for this. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="contactor"></span>**Contactor**: A heavy-duty relay that switches the main battery current; on Marvin Bot the e-stop chain opens it. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/), [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>
- <span id="control-hierarchy"></span>**Control hierarchy**: Stacking control loops at different rates, each slower loop setting targets for a faster one below it: VLA at a few hertz, policy at 50 Hz, joint PD and current loops at kilohertz. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/)</span>
- <span id="copper-loss"></span>**Copper loss**: Heat made by current flowing through the motor's copper windings. It grows with the current squared, so doubling the torque roughly quadruples the heat. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>
- <span id="coulomb-friction"></span>**Coulomb friction**: A constant resisting torque that opposes motion regardless of speed, from gear teeth and seals. <span class="on">On: [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="coupled-joint-limit"></span>**Coupled joint limit**: A joint limit that depends on another joint's angle; on Marvin Bot the shoulder roll may go below 0 degrees only with the arm swung forward. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/), [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/), [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="crank"></span>**Crank**: A short arm fixed to a motor shaft; its tip moves on a circle and pushes or pulls a rod. <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/)</span>
- <span id="curriculum"></span>**Curriculum**: Training that starts with easy tasks (flat ground, slow speeds) and makes them harder as the policy improves. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="cycloidal-drive"></span>**Cycloidal drive**: A gearbox in which a lobed disc rolls inside a ring of pins, sharing load over many lobes; compact and shock-tolerant. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>

## D

- <span id="damped-least-squares"></span>**Damped least squares**: A way to solve inverse kinematics step by step: nudge every joint in the direction that moves the foot (or hand) towards its target, with a little damping so the steps stay small and stable near awkward poses where the leg is straight. <span class="on">On: [00 Marvin Bot in 3-D](../00-3d-model/)</span>
- <span id="damping"></span><b>Damping (kd)</b>: The "D" setting of a PD controller (in N·m·s/rad): how strongly a joint pushes back against moving fast, like a door closer. More damping means less wobble but slower motion. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="damping-mode"></span>**Damping mode**: All joints commanded to resist motion like dampers, with no position target, so a falling robot crumples instead of thrashing. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>
- <span id="damping-ratio"></span>**Damping ratio**: A single number that says how a spring-like joint settles: below 1 it overshoots and wobbles, at exactly 1 it arrives as fast as possible without overshooting, above 1 it creeps in slowly. Computed as kd / (2 sqrt(kp J)). <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="dc-dc-converter"></span>**DC-DC converter**: An electronic circuit that converts one DC voltage to another, such as 48 V to 12 V. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="decoupled-control"></span>**Decoupled control**: Splitting a humanoid's control into a learned lower-body balance/locomotion policy and a separate arm controller such as IK. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="defence-in-depth"></span>**Defence in depth**: Several independent safety layers, so a fault one layer misses is caught by the next. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>
- <span id="degree-of-freedom"></span>**Degree of freedom**: One independent number needed to describe a configuration, such as one joint angle. A rigid body in space has 6. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/)</span>
- <span id="depth-of-discharge"></span>**Depth of discharge**: The share of a battery's capacity that is actually used between charges. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="developer-kit"></span>**Developer kit**: A ready-to-use board sold for development, with the module, carrier board, cooling and ports. Marvin Bot uses the Jetson AGX Orin 64 GB developer kit. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="diffusion-model"></span>**Diffusion model**: A generative neural network that produces data (images, motions, actions) by gradually removing noise from a random start, guided by a condition such as a text prompt. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="discount-factor"></span>**Discount factor**: A number γ between 0 and 1 that sets how much future rewards count; 0.99 at 50 Hz means the policy looks about 2 s ahead. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="disparity"></span>**Disparity**: How far a point shifts between a stereo camera's two images, in pixels; depth is focal length times baseline divided by disparity. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="distal-mass"></span>**Distal mass**: Mass far from the joint that swings a limb, such as motors at the ankle. It costs inertia as the square of its distance. <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/), [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/)</span>
- <span id="divergent-component-of-motion"></span>**Divergent component of motion**: Another name for the capture point used in walking control: the part of the body's motion that keeps running away (like a falling broom) unless the feet push in the right place. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/)</span>
- <span id="domain-randomisation"></span>**Domain randomisation**: Varying physical parameters such as friction, mass and delays across simulated episodes so the policy works over a range that includes the real robot. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/), [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="dorsiflexion"></span>**Dorsiflexion**: Bending the ankle so the toes move up towards the shin (positive ankle pitch on Marvin Bot). <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/)</span>
- <span id="duty-cycle"></span>**Duty cycle**: The share of a cycle during which a load is applied, here the stance share of a step. <span class="on">On: [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/)</span>

## E

- <span id="e-stop"></span>**E-stop**: Emergency stop: a latching control that removes hazardous power; on Marvin Bot it opens the main contactor. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>
- <span id="effective-mass"></span>**Effective mass**: How heavy a moving part of the robot feels to whatever it hits. A long arm swung from the shoulder can feel lighter or heavier at the hand than its real mass, depending on how its mass is spread out. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>
- <span id="encoder"></span>**Encoder**: A sensor that measures a joint's angle; RobStride actuators use absolute magnetic encoders. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="episode"></span>**Episode**: One run of a simulated robot from its start until it falls, is reset or times out. Training averages over many thousands of episodes running in parallel. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="execution-horizon"></span>**Execution horizon**: How many actions of each chunk are executed before switching to the next chunk. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="exploded-view"></span>**Exploded view**: A picture of an assembly with its pieces pulled apart along the directions they fit together, so you can see which part attaches to which. <span class="on">On: [00 Marvin Bot in 3-D](../00-3d-model/)</span>
- <span id="extended-identifier"></span>**Extended identifier**: The longer, 29-bit form of a CAN message's identifier, the label that says what a message is and sets its priority (the standard form has 11 bits). Marvin Bot's RobStride motors use the 29-bit form. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>

## F

- <span id="far-side-bearing"></span>**Far-side bearing**: A second bearing on the opposite side of a joint from the motor output, so the link is supported on both sides and side loads do not bend thin plates. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="field-weakening"></span>**Field weakening**: A motor-control technique that partly cancels the magnets' field to run above the normal top speed, at the cost of torque and extra heat. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>
- <span id="field-oriented-control"></span>**Field-oriented control**: The method a brushless motor driver uses to steer current through the motor's three coils thousands of times a second, so the motor produces exactly the torque that was asked for. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/)</span>
- <span id="floating-base"></span>**Floating base**: A robot body, like a humanoid's pelvis, that is not bolted down; its 6 degrees of freedom are not driven by any motor. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/)</span>
- <span id="flow-matching"></span>**Flow matching**: A way of training a model to produce data (here, short sequences of robot motion) by learning how to nudge random noise, step by step, towards a realistic sample. It needs only a few steps, so it is fast enough for robots. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="foot-sliding"></span><b>Foot sliding (skate)</b>: Horizontal movement of a foot that should be planted, a common artefact of generated or retargeted motion, measured in cm/s. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="forward-kinematics"></span>**Forward kinematics**: Computing where each link of the robot is, and how it is oriented, from the joint angles and segment lengths, working outwards joint by joint from the pelvis. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/), [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/), [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="frame"></span>**Frame**: One CAN message: identifier, control bits, up to 8 data bytes (64 in CAN FD), checksum and acknowledgement. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="friction-cone"></span>**Friction cone**: The range of directions a foot (or finger) can push in without slipping: the sideways force can be at most μ times the pressing force, which traces out a cone around the surface normal. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="cost-to-replicate"></span>**Full cost to replicate**: What someone starting from nothing would pay for all parts, with owned items still counted at their current price. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>

## G

- <span id="gae"></span>**GAE**: Generalised Advantage Estimation: a recipe used in PPO training for scoring how good each action was, by blending short-term evidence (less noisy but more biased) with long-term evidence (more noisy). A parameter λ sets the mix. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="gantry"></span>**Gantry**: A frame with a harness above the robot. It catches the robot before it hits the floor during early standing and walking tests. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/), [22 Build phases and roadmap](../22-roadmap/)</span>
- <span id="gear-ratio"></span>**Gear ratio**: How many turns the motor makes for one turn of the output. A ratio of 9 (RS04, RS03, RS06) multiplies torque by about 9 and divides speed by 9; it also multiplies the rotor's inertia felt at the joint by 81 (the ratio squared). <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/), [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="gear-stage"></span>**Gear stage**: An extra reduction between motor and joint: multiplies torque by the ratio and divides speed by it. <span class="on">On: [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/)</span>
- <span id="gs-usb"></span>**gs\_usb**: An open standard for USB-to-CAN adapters (used by candleLight adapters); Linux has a driver for it built in, so no extra software is needed. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="gyroscope"></span>**Gyroscope**: A sensor that measures angular velocity (how fast something rotates) about three axes. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>

## H

- <span id="harmonic-drive"></span>**Harmonic drive**: A strain-wave gearbox giving high ratios (30:1 to 160:1) with no backlash, but hard to backdrive and sensitive to shock loads. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>

## I

- <span id="ideal-diode-or"></span>**Ideal-diode OR**: A circuit that lets two power sources feed the same device, so if one is removed the other takes over instantly, without either one pushing current back into the other. It uses transistors that act like one-way valves with almost no loss. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="impulse"></span>**Impulse**: Force times the time it acts (N·s); a short push of impulse J changes the CoM velocity by J divided by the mass. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/)</span>
- <span id="imu"></span>**IMU**: Inertial measurement unit: a gyroscope and an accelerometer that measure rotation rate and the direction of gravity. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/), [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="inertia-tensor"></span>**Inertia tensor**: Nine numbers (a 3 x 3 table, in kg·m²) that describe how hard a part is to spin about each direction through its centre of mass. A long thin rod, for example, spins easily about its length and hard end over end. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="integrator"></span>**Integrator**: The rule that turns accelerations into new velocities and positions each step, such as semi-implicit Euler or Runge-Kutta 4. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="internal-resistance"></span>**Internal resistance**: The small resistance inside every cell that makes its voltage sag under load and rise under charge. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="inverse-kinematics"></span>**Inverse kinematics**: The reverse problem: finding joint angles that put a link at a desired position and orientation. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/), [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>

## J

- <span id="jacobian"></span>**Jacobian**: A table of numbers that says how fast a point such as the hand or foot moves when each joint turns a little. Used the other way round, it converts a force at the hand or foot into the torque each joint must supply. <span class="on">On: [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/), [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/), [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="jitter"></span>**Jitter**: Variation in timing from one cycle to the next. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="joint"></span>**Joint**: A connection between a parent and a child link that allows motion, here a hinge about one axis with limits. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="joint-limit"></span><b>Joint limit (range)</b>: The smallest and largest angle a joint is allowed to reach, set by where parts would collide or a linkage runs out of travel. Marvin Bot's ranges are written once in description/robot.yaml. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/), [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>

## K

- <span id="keep-alive-battery"></span>**Keep-alive battery**: A small battery that powers only the computer so it stays on while the main pack is swapped. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="keypoint"></span>**Keypoint**: A tracked point on a body, such as a hip, knee, wrist or shoulder, used to compare two bodies' poses. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="kinematic-chain"></span>**Kinematic chain**: Links connected by joints. In a serial chain such as a leg, each link is carried by the one before it. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/)</span>

## L

- <span id="landed-cost"></span>**Landed cost**: The price of a part once shipping, import duty and taxes are added. The Marvin Bot BOM does not include these. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="latency"></span>**Latency**: The delay between something happening (a tilt, a command) and the system acting on it; it adds up stage by stage through sensors, computer, bus and driver. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/), [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="lead-time"></span>**Lead time**: The time between ordering a part and having it in hand. It does not shrink however many hours you work. <span class="on">On: [22 Build phases and roadmap](../22-roadmap/)</span>
- <span id="least-squares"></span>**Least squares**: Choosing model parameters that minimise the sum of squared differences between the model's predictions and the measurements. <span class="on">On: [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="lever-arm"></span>**Lever arm**: The perpendicular distance from a joint axis to the line along which a force acts. <span class="on">On: [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/)</span>
- <span id="linear-inverted-pendulum"></span>**Linear inverted pendulum**: The simplest model of a walking robot: all the mass in one point at a fixed height, balanced on a weightless stick leg, like a broom balanced on your hand. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/)</span>
- <span id="link"></span>**Link**: One rigid body of a robot model, such as a thigh or a foot. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="linkage-drive"></span>**Linkage drive**: Moving finger joints through rigid bars from motors at the finger base or in the palm. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="load-path"></span>**Load path**: The chain of parts a force travels through, for example from the foot to the pelvis during a landing. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>

## M

- <span id="made-part"></span>**Made part**: A part designed in the project and produced from raw material, by CNC machining or 3D printing. The BOM prices only its inputs: stock, filament, tools or a machining service. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="mass-budget"></span>**Mass budget**: A list of every part of a machine with its mass and how that mass is known (estimate, CAD, datasheet, weighed), summed and checked against a target and a limit. <span class="on">On: [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/)</span>
- <span id="massively-parallel-simulation"></span>**Massively parallel simulation**: Running thousands of copies of the robot at once on one GPU to collect reinforcement-learning experience quickly. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="mirror-rule"></span>**Mirror rule**: How the right side is built from the left: y coordinates negated, roll and yaw axes negated, so equal angles mean the same motion on both sides. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="mit-mode"></span>**MIT mode**: A way of commanding a motor in which each message gives a target angle and speed plus how stiff and how damped the joint should be (kp and kd) and an extra torque; the motor driver then works out the torque itself, very fast. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/), [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="mjcf"></span>**MJCF**: MuJoCo's own robot file format (XML). It holds what a URDF holds plus simulator settings such as motor models, joint friction and contact behaviour. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="module"></span><b>Module (assembly)</b>: A section of the robot that is built and tested on its own and then joined to the rest at a few bolted flanges and connectors: Marvin Bot has seven (torso core, pelvis, two legs, two arms, head), plus the battery and the optional shells. <span class="on">On: [00 Marvin Bot in 3-D](../00-3d-model/)</span>
- <span id="moment-arm"></span>**Moment arm**: The perpendicular distance from a joint axis to the line along which a force acts (for a weight, the horizontal distance); torque equals force times moment arm. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="moment-of-inertia"></span>**Moment of inertia**: A body's resistance to angular acceleration about an axis: the sum of each mass times its distance from the axis squared, in kg·m². <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/), [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/)</span>
- <span id="motion-tracking"></span>**Motion tracking**: Training a policy to follow a reference motion clip, rewarded for staying close to it. A new skill is then a new clip rather than new reward code. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>

## N

- <span id="natural-frequency"></span>**Natural frequency**: How fast a joint held by a PD controller would naturally swing back and forth, like a mass on a spring: stiffer settings or lighter limbs give a higher frequency. Computed as sqrt(kp / J) in rad/s (divide by 2π for Hz). <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="neutral-axis"></span>**Neutral axis**: In a bent beam, the line where the material is neither stretched nor squeezed: on one side it stretches, on the other it compresses. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="no-load-speed"></span>**No-load speed**: The fastest an actuator turns with no load at its rated voltage, where back-EMF uses up the whole supply voltage. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>
- <span id="noise-density"></span>**Noise density**: How noisy a sensor is, given in a form that does not depend on how fast you read it. The total noise you see grows with the square root of the bandwidth (roughly, how fast the readings are allowed to change). <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="null-space"></span>**Null space**: The joint motions that leave a task untouched. With your hand resting on a table, you can still move your elbow: that elbow motion is in the null space of the hand task, and a controller can use it for a lower-priority goal. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>

## O

- <span id="observation"></span>**Observation**: Everything the policy is shown at each step: for Marvin Bot's walking policy, the body's angular velocity, which way gravity points, the speed command, the joint angles and speeds, and its own previous action. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/), [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="on-policy"></span>**On-policy**: A kind of learning that only learns from experience collected by the current version of the policy; once the policy is updated, the old experience is thrown away and fresh experience is collected. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="onnx"></span>**ONNX**: Open Neural Network Exchange: a portable file format for trained networks, used to move a policy from the training code to the robot. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/)</span>
- <span id="overshoot"></span>**Overshoot**: How far a step response goes past its target, as a percentage of the step. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>

## P

- <span id="parallel-mechanism"></span>**Parallel mechanism**: A mechanism in which several actuators drive the same output through separate paths, so each load is shared. <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/)</span>
- <span id="pass-check"></span>**Pass check**: A test at the end of a build phase. It decides whether the project moves on or fixes something first. <span class="on">On: [22 Build phases and roadmap](../22-roadmap/)</span>
- <span id="pd-control"></span>**PD control**: Proportional-derivative control, the simplest joint controller: the motor pushes towards the target angle with a strength kp (like a spring) and resists speed with a strength kd (like a damper). Torque = kp × (target − angle) − kd × speed. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="peak-torque"></span>**Peak torque**: The most torque an actuator can give in short bursts, limited by current; it overheats in seconds if held. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/), [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/)</span>
- <span id="penalty-contact"></span>**Penalty contact**: Contact modelled as a stiff spring and damper that pushes back in proportion to how far bodies overlap. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="physics-engine"></span>**Physics engine**: Software that steps a model forward in time: detects contacts, computes constraint forces and integrates motion. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="pinch-grasp"></span>**Pinch grasp**: Holding an object between fingertip and thumb, where friction at the two contacts must support its weight. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="pocketing"></span>**Pocketing**: Milling recesses into a plate down to a thin floor, leaving full-depth ribs, to save mass while keeping bending strength. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="policy"></span>**Policy**: The trained neural-network controller: every control tick (50 Hz on Marvin Bot) it maps what the robot observes to the actions it takes, here joint position targets. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/), [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/), [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="power-grasp"></span>**Power grasp**: Holding an object wrapped in the curled fingers and palm, so geometry carries the load rather than fingertip friction alone. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="ppo"></span>**PPO**: Proximal Policy Optimisation: the trial-and-error learning method used to train Marvin Bot's walking. Its key idea is to change the policy only a little at each update, so training stays stable instead of lurching between behaviours. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="precharge"></span>**Precharge**: Filling the motor drivers' input capacitors slowly through a resistor before the main contactor closes, to avoid a huge inrush current. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="price-basis"></span>**Price basis**: A note saying where a price came from and when: a dated listing, an unverified reseller page or an estimate. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="price-per-newton-metre"></span>**Price per N·m**: Actuator price divided by its peak or continuous torque. It gives a first comparison of value across sizes and brands. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="projected-gravity"></span>**Projected gravity**: The gravity direction expressed in the pelvis frame; the policy uses it instead of tilt angles. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="proprioceptive-actuation"></span>**Proprioceptive actuation**: Controlling contact forces using only the actuator's own current and position sensing, without force sensors; enabled by low gear ratios. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>
- <span id="push-rod"></span>**Push rod**: A rigid rod of fixed length that transmits force between a crank and the driven part through joints at each end. <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/)</span>
- <span id="push-off"></span>**Push-off**: The moment at the end of a step when the back foot lifts its heel and pushes through the toes before leaving the ground. On a stair it is when the leading leg starts carrying the whole body. <span class="on">On: [00 Marvin Bot in 3-D](../00-3d-model/)</span>

## Q

- <span id="quadratic-program"></span><b>Quadratic program (QP)</b>: A kind of optimisation problem that computers solve very quickly: minimise a sum of squared errors while respecting limits written as simple linear rules. Classical whole-body controllers solve one every control cycle. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="quantity-tier"></span>**Quantity tier**: A lower unit price for ordering at least a set number of one item, such as Seeed's 10+ price for RobStride actuators. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="quasi-direct-drive"></span>**Quasi-direct drive**: An actuator with a large-diameter motor and a single low-ratio gear stage (about 6:1 to 10:1), keeping low reflected inertia and backdrivability while giving enough torque. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/), [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>
- <span id="quasi-static"></span>**Quasi-static**: Treating a movement as a series of still poses, so only gravity (and the floor's push) is counted and accelerations are ignored. A good first estimate for slow movements; real fast movements need more torque. <span class="on">On: [00 Marvin Bot in 3-D](../00-3d-model/)</span>
- <span id="quasi-static-contact"></span>**Quasi-static contact**: Contact in which a body part is clamped and pressed, as opposed to a brief transient impact. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>

## R

- <span id="rated-torque"></span>**Rated (continuous) torque**: The torque an actuator can hold indefinitely without its winding passing the temperature limit, measured with a specified heat sink. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/), [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/), [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/), [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="reduction-stage"></span>**Reduction stage**: An extra gear, belt or linkage between motor and joint that multiplies torque and divides speed by its ratio. <span class="on">On: [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/)</span>
- <span id="reference-motion"></span>**Reference motion**: The recorded or generated motion a tracker should follow: where each body part is at every moment. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="reflected-inertia"></span>**Reflected inertia**: The motor rotor's inertia as felt at the joint: N² times the rotor inertia for a gear ratio N. It adds to impact forces. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/), [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/), [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="regenerative-braking"></span>**Regenerative braking**: A motor slowing a moving load acts as a generator and pushes energy back onto the power bus. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="reinforcement-learning"></span>**Reinforcement learning**: Learning a behaviour by trial and error: an agent acts, receives a numerical reward, and adjusts itself to collect more reward over time. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="retargeting"></span>**Retargeting**: Converting a motion from one body (usually a person) to another (the robot) with different sizes, joints and limits, typically by optimising the robot's joint angles to match scaled keypoints. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="return"></span>**Return**: The sum of future rewards from a moment on, with later rewards counted less by the discount factor. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="revolute-joint"></span>**Revolute joint**: A hinge that allows rotation about one axis and nothing else: one degree of freedom. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/)</span>
- <span id="reward"></span>**Reward**: A number the training environment gives the policy after every step to score what it just did (for example, positive for following the commanded speed, negative for wasting torque). Reinforcement learning adjusts the policy to collect as much total reward as possible. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/), [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="reward-hacking"></span>**Reward hacking**: When an optimiser finds behaviour that scores well on the reward but is not what the designer intended, such as crouching or standing still. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="reward-kernel"></span>**Reward kernel**: A formula that turns an error (how far off the robot is) into a reward between 0 and 1: exactly 1 when the error is zero, about 0.37 when the error equals a chosen tolerance σ, and almost 0 beyond twice that tolerance. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="riser"></span>**Riser**: The vertical height of one stair step. <span class="on">On: [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/)</span>
- <span id="risk-register"></span>**Risk register**: A list of things that could be wrong, each with how and when it will be checked. <span class="on">On: [22 Build phases and roadmap](../22-roadmap/)</span>
- <span id="risk-driven-development"></span>**Risk-driven development**: Planning work so the biggest remaining uncertainty is tested first, with the cheapest build that can expose it. <span class="on">On: [22 Build phases and roadmap](../22-roadmap/)</span>
- <span id="rms-torque"></span>**RMS torque**: Root-mean-square torque over a cycle: the steady torque that would heat the motor as much as the real varying torque. <span class="on">On: [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/)</span>
- <span id="robot-description"></span>**Robot description**: Files that tell software what a robot is made of: links, joints, masses, inertias, shapes and limits. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="rod-end"></span>**Rod end**: A ball joint at the end of a rod that lets it swivel in all directions while carrying push and pull forces. <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/)</span>
- <span id="root-path"></span>**Root path**: The trajectory of the hips over the ground (position and heading per frame), used as a constraint so a generated motion moves at a chosen speed and direction. <span class="on">On: [17 Motion tracking: Kimodo, retargeting and BeyondMimic](../17-motion-tracking/)</span>
- <span id="ros2-control"></span>**ros2\_control**: Part of ROS 2 (a popular robot software framework) for talking to hardware in a tight, regular loop: it reads sensors, runs controllers and writes motor commands each cycle. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/)</span>

## S

- <span id="safety-factor"></span>**Safety factor**: The material's yield strength divided by the peak stress in the part. 1 means the part just starts to bend permanently. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="sagittal-plane"></span>**Sagittal plane**: The side-view plane that divides the body into left and right; forward-back and up-down motion lie in it. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/)</span>
- <span id="second-moment-of-area"></span>**Second moment of area**: A number (in mm⁴) that captures how far a beam's cross-section material sits from its centre line. Material far from the centre resists bending much more, which is why I-beams and tubes are stiff for their weight. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="semi-implicit-euler"></span>**Semi-implicit Euler**: An integrator that updates the velocity first, then moves with the new velocity; cheap, and unlike plain Euler it does not steadily gain energy over long runs. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="serial-bus-servo"></span>**Serial bus servo**: A hobby-class servo with an ID that shares a daisy-chained serial line with others and reports its position and load. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="serial-mechanism"></span>**Serial mechanism**: A chain of joints each driven by its own actuator, one after another, like a typical robot arm. <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/)</span>
- <span id="settling-time"></span>**Settling time**: Time after a step until the joint stays within a band (here 2% of the step) of its final value. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="sim-to-real-gap"></span>**Sim-to-real gap**: The difference between how the simulated robot and the real robot respond to the same commands, which can make a policy that works in simulation fail on hardware. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/), [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/), [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/), [22 Build phases and roadmap](../22-roadmap/)</span>
- <span id="sim-to-sim"></span>**Sim-to-sim**: Checking a policy trained in one simulator in a second, independent one (Marvin Bot: trained on Newton, checked in MuJoCo), as a cheap proxy for the real world. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="sim-to-sim-check"></span>**Sim-to-sim check**: Running a policy trained in one simulator in a different one (Marvin Bot: Newton to MuJoCo) to catch dependence on one engine's quirks. <span class="on">On: [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="single-source-of-truth"></span>**Single source of truth**: Keeping each fact in one place and generating every other copy from it, so copies cannot disagree. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="singularity"></span>**Singularity**: A pose where joint axes line up so the mechanism loses a direction of motion; near it, small motions in that direction need very large joint speeds. Also called gimbal lock for three-axis joints. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/), [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/)</span>
- <span id="socketcan"></span>**SocketCAN**: The part of Linux that makes a CAN adapter look like a network connection, so programs can send and receive CAN messages with ordinary networking tools. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="soft-contact"></span>**Soft contact**: How MuJoCo handles contact: instead of perfectly rigid surfaces, it lets bodies overlap very slightly and push back like a stiff, damped cushion, which keeps the simulation stable. Settings called solref and solimp tune the cushion. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="spares"></span>**Spares**: Extra units bought beyond what the robot uses, so that one failure does not stop the project for weeks. Marvin Bot keeps one spare RS04, RS03 and RS06. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="square-cube-law"></span>**Square-cube law**: When a body is scaled up by s, its areas grow as s squared and its volume and mass as s cubed, so weight outgrows strength. Joint torques grow about as s to the fourth. <span class="on">On: [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/)</span>
- <span id="staged-buying"></span>**Staged buying**: Buying parts one build phase at a time, so a failed test costs only that phase's parts. <span class="on">On: [21 Bill of materials and costs](../21-bom-costs/)</span>
- <span id="stall-torque"></span>**Stall torque**: The torque a motor or servo produces when held still at full power; it can only be sustained for a moment before overheating. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="stance"></span><b>Stance (default pose)</b>: The standing pose the walking policies act around and start from: for Marvin Bot hip pitch 6°, knee 15°, ankle pitch 9°, with the pelvis 0.694 m above the floor. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/), [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="state-of-charge"></span>**State of charge**: How full a battery is, from 0% to 100%. <span class="on">On: [09 Power and battery](../09-power-battery/)</span>
- <span id="steady-state-error"></span>**Steady-state error**: The offset left after settling, e.g. gravity torque divided by kp for a PD loop. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="stiffness"></span><b>Stiffness (kp)</b>: The "P" setting of a PD controller (in N·m/rad): how hard a joint pulls back towards its target for each radian it is off, like the strength of a spring. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="stop-category"></span>**Stop category**: IEC 60204-1's names for how a machine stops: 0 cut power at once, 1 controlled stop then cut power, 2 controlled stop with power kept. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>
- <span id="stress-concentration"></span>**Stress concentration**: A local rise in stress at a hole, notch or sharp corner, often 2-3 times the nominal value, expressed as a factor Kt. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>
- <span id="support-polygon"></span>**Support polygon**: The smallest convex outline containing every point where the feet touch the ground. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/)</span>
- <span id="system-1-system-2"></span>**System 1 / System 2**: GR00T's split into two parts: a slower part that looks at images and reads the instruction to understand the scene (System 2, "thinking"), and a faster part that turns that understanding into motion (System 1, "acting"). <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="system-identification"></span>**System identification**: Measuring a real system's response to known inputs and fitting the parameters of a model so it reproduces that response. <span class="on">On: [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>

## T

- <span id="teacher-student-training"></span>**Teacher-student training**: Train a "teacher" policy in simulation that is allowed to see perfect information (exact terrain, true speeds), then train a "student" that copies the teacher's actions while seeing only what real sensors provide. <span class="on">On: [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="technology-readiness-level"></span>**Technology readiness level**: NASA's 1-9 scale of how proven a technology is, from basic principles up to a system proven in real use. <span class="on">On: [22 Build phases and roadmap](../22-roadmap/)</span>
- <span id="teleoperation"></span>**Teleoperation**: A person controlling the robot remotely (VR headset, exoskeleton, joystick), often to record demonstrations. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="tendon-drive"></span>**Tendon drive**: Moving finger joints by cables pulled by motors placed elsewhere, usually in the forearm or palm. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="tensorrt"></span>**TensorRT**: NVIDIA's inference optimiser and runtime that compiles a trained network for faster execution on a GPU or Jetson. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="termination"></span>**Termination**: A 120 ohm resistor at each end of a CAN bus that stops signal reflections. <span class="on">On: [10 CAN buses and bandwidth](../10-can-bus/)</span>
- <span id="thermal-runaway"></span>**Thermal runaway**: A lithium-ion cell heating itself uncontrollably, venting flammable gas and possibly igniting. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>
- <span id="thermal-time-constant"></span>**Thermal time constant**: How long a body takes to approach its final temperature (about 63% of the way after one time constant); windings take seconds, housings minutes. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/)</span>
- <span id="timestep"></span>**Timestep**: The fixed time between simulation updates; 2 ms in Marvin Bot's MuJoCo checks, 5 ms in Isaac Lab training. <span class="on">On: [14 Simulation: MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton and PhysX](../14-simulation/)</span>
- <span id="tops"></span>**TOPS**: Trillions of operations per second: the headline figure for an AI chip's speed. It is usually quoted for low-precision maths and with a trick (sparsity) that doubles it, so real-world throughput is often half or less. <span class="on">On: [11 Sensors and compute](../11-sensors-compute/)</span>
- <span id="torque"></span>**Torque**: The turning effect of a force: force times the perpendicular distance from the axis, in N·m. <span class="on">On: [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/), [07 Mass budget and proportions](../07-mass-proportions/)</span>
- <span id="torque-constant"></span>**Torque constant**: Torque produced per ampere of motor current (N·m/A). RobStride quotes it at the gearbox output, e.g. 2.1 N·m/A for the RS04. <span class="on">On: [04 Actuators: quasi-direct drive, torque, speed and heat](../04-actuators/), [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="torque-speed-curve"></span>**Torque-speed line**: The motor's available torque falling from peak at standstill to zero at no-load speed; Isaac Lab's DC motor model uses a straight line. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
- <span id="transmission-angle"></span>**Transmission angle**: The angle between a crank and its rod; its sine (1 when square, 0 when in line) is the fraction of crank torque that becomes useful rod force. <span class="on">On: [06 The parallel-linkage ankle](../06-ankle-linkage/)</span>
- <span id="tread"></span>**Tread**: The horizontal depth of one stair step. <span class="on">On: [05 Leg loads and stairs](../05-leg-loads-stairs/)</span>

## U

- <span id="underactuation"></span>**Underactuation**: Having fewer motors than joints; linkages, tendons or springs couple joints so one motor curls several and fingers wrap around objects. <span class="on">On: [19 Hands](../19-hands/)</span>
- <span id="urdf"></span>**URDF**: Unified Robot Description Format, the XML robot format of ROS; holds links, joints, inertia, visual and collision shapes and joint limits. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="usd"></span>**USD**: Universal Scene Description (OpenUSD): the 3-D scene file format that NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab load. Extra physics information added to it describes the robot's bodies and joints. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>

## V

- <span id="value-function"></span>**Value function**: A helper network (the critic) that guesses how much total reward the robot will collect from a given situation. It is used only during training, to judge whether actions were better or worse than expected; it never runs on the robot. <span class="on">On: [16 Reinforcement learning for walking](../16-rl-walking/)</span>
- <span id="viscous-friction"></span>**Viscous friction**: Resistance proportional to speed, in N·m·s/rad. <span class="on">On: [20 Sim-to-real and system identification](../20-sim-to-real/)</span>
- <span id="vision-language-action-model"></span>**Vision-language-action model**: A large neural network that takes camera images and a text instruction and outputs short chunks of robot motion; GR00T N1.7 is the one planned for Marvin Bot. <span class="on">On: [03 System architecture: from battery to policy](../03-architecture/)</span>
- <span id="visual-geometry"></span>**Visual geometry**: Shapes or meshes used only for drawing the robot; they have no effect on the physics. <span class="on">On: [13 The robot description: one source of truth](../13-description/)</span>
- <span id="vla"></span><b>VLA (vision-language-action model)</b>: A neural network that takes camera images, a text instruction and robot state and outputs robot actions. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>

## W

- <span id="watchdog"></span>**Watchdog**: A circuit that trips if it stops receiving regular heartbeat signals from the controller; here a relay that opens the contactor. <span class="on">On: [12 Safety](../12-safety/)</span>
- <span id="whole-body-control"></span>**Whole-body control**: Deciding all joint commands together so several goals (balance, hands, head, limits) are met at once, in order of priority. <span class="on">On: [18 Whole-body control and VLA models](../18-wbc-vla/)</span>
- <span id="workspace"></span>**Workspace**: The set of positions (and orientations) a link can reach within the joint ranges. <span class="on">On: [01 Degrees of freedom and kinematic chains](../01-dof-kinematics/)</span>

## Y

- <span id="yield-strength"></span>**Yield strength**: The stress at which a material starts to deform permanently. <span class="on">On: [08 Structure and code-CAD](../08-structure-cad/)</span>

## Z

- <span id="zero-moment-point"></span>**Zero-moment point**: The point on the ground where the combined push of gravity and the body's motion acts, with no tendency to tip about it. While it stays inside the area under the feet the robot does not topple; on flat ground it is the same as the centre of pressure. <span class="on">On: [02 Balance: centre of mass, support polygon, ZMP and capture point](../02-balance/)</span>
- <span id="zero-order-hold"></span>**Zero-order hold**: A digital controller keeps its last output constant until the next update. <span class="on">On: [15 PD control, gains and armature](../15-pd-control/)</span>
