# Bill of materials

`bom.csv` lists the parts to buy, by phase; `tools/bom_summary.py` totals it. `build_parts.csv`
and `build_fasteners.csv` are generated by `python -m cad.export` from the CAD.

Prices and links are indicative only: they are what a listing showed on the date given in
`price_basis`, often from a reseller and not confirmed with the maker (marked unverified). They
are not quotes or endorsements; the project has no affiliation with any vendor and earns
nothing from these links. Check current prices, specifications and import costs before buying.
Owned items are counted at $0.
