# Bill of materials

`bom.csv` lists the parts to buy, by phase; `tools/bom_summary.py` totals it and checks it.
`sourcing.csv` lists where each researched item is sold (official store first, then
distributors), with links, prices, availability and the date checked. `build_parts.csv` and
`build_fasteners.csv` are generated by `python -m cad.export` from the CAD.

The CSVs are the source of truth. `python tools/build_bom_xlsx.py` (needs `pip install -e
".[bom]"`) turns them into a spreadsheet, `outputs/bom.xlsx`. It has one sheet per group with line
totals as formulas, a summary per group and per phase, the sourcing table and the CAD's part
and bolt lists. The spreadsheet is generated, so it is not committed.

## Cost

At October 2026 prices the full robot comes to about **$17.5k** (`tools/bom_summary.py`). Phase 2
is about $1.0k, phase 3 $3.2k, phase 4 $9.1k, phase 5 $3.8k and phase 6 $0.4k. By group:
actuators $6.2k, compute and sensors $4.7k (the Jetson AGX Orin 64 GB developer kit alone is
$3,499, up from $1,999 before NVIDIA's July 2026 price rise), structure and materials $2.6k,
power $1.6k, and the rest $2.4k. Owned items (the developer kit, the two AmazingHand kits and a
USB IMU) carry their current price, so the total is what a replicator pays. This build already
owns $3.7k of it and has about $13.8k still to buy.

Not counted:
- **Desktop GPU for training (optional).** Isaac Lab training and GR00T fine-tuning want an RTX
  card with 24-32 GB. The RTX 5090 lists at $1,999, but in late 2026 it sold for about $4,000 or
  more. A rented cloud GPU works too.
- The desktop CNC mill and 3D printer (R60), shipping, import duties and tariffs. Most actuators
  and the hand kits ship from China.

## Price basis

Prices and links are indicative only. Each one is what a listing showed on the date given in
`price_basis`, often from a reseller and not confirmed with the maker (marked unverified or
"estimate"). Lines marked "estimate" have no listing behind them yet: online machining, the IMU,
the charger and most power and wiring parts. They are not quotes or endorsements. The project
has no affiliation with any vendor and earns nothing from these links. Check current prices,
specifications and import costs before buying.
